Hades II 4:3 Patcher

Makes Hades II's boon choices and character conversations fill 4:3 screens (Retroid Pocket, DroidDeck/GameNative, 4:3 monitors) so their text is readable. Gameplay is unchanged.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your RoomLogic.lua is never uploaded and never leaves this device.

Install it

  1. Rename your original RoomLogic.lua to RoomLogic.lua.bak. Keep it as your backup.
  2. Put the downloaded RoomLogic.lua into the same Content/Scripts folder.
  3. Run the game at your screen's 4:3 resolution in Fullscreen (1280x960 on the Retroid Pocket Nova). After a game update, patch the new RoomLogic.lua again.
